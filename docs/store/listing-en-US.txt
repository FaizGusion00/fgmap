Full description (1040/4000)
FGMaps is navigation built for Malaysia. Routes are calculated on your phone — including offline mode after you download the map pack.

KEY FEATURES
• Turn-by-turn navigation with Malay and English voice guidance
• Offline maps for Malaysia (download once, navigate without signal)
• Up to 10 route options: recommended, fastest, shortest and no-toll
• Toll and fuel cost estimates before you leave
• Search places, addresses, coordinates or Maps links
• Home & Work shortcuts plus nearby search (fuel, food, mosque, hospital and more)
• Light, Dark, AMOLED black and Sun (automatic) themes
• Emergency 999 / 112 button always close by

FREE & PRIVACY
FGMaps is free. No account, no ads, no tracking and no subscription. Trip data and search history stay on your device. You can delete all local data in Settings.

Note: toll estimates are estimates based on available rates; not official bills. Always drive safely and follow traffic laws.
